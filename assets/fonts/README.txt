SELF-HOSTED FONTS
=================
The .woff2 files here are served from this site's own origin. These pages
make no external requests of any kind: no Google Fonts, no CDN, no
analytics, no scripts, and every image is a local file.

The @font-face rules are at the top of assets/css/style.css and point here
with relative paths (../fonts/...). Where a face is variable, one file
covers the whole weight axis.

6 files, 187 KB on disk.
Only latin and latin-ext are included, so a visitor typically downloads
about half of that; unicode-range decides which.

Regenerated with _build/selfhost_fonts.py from:
  https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Cormorant+Garamond:ital,wght@0,300..700;1,300..700&family=Karla:wght@200..800&display=swap
